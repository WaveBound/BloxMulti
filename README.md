# BloxMulti

BloxMulti is a multi-account launcher for Roblox that makes it easy to manage multiple accounts and launch games quickly.

## Features

*   **Multi-Account Support**: Add as many accounts as you need and switch between them instantly.
*   **Isolated Sessions**: Every account has its own browser session, so you stay logged in to all of them at once.
*   **Multi-Launch**: Select multiple accounts and launch them all into a game with one click.
*   **Saved Games**: Save Place IDs and private server codes so you don't have to keep copy-pasting them.
*   **Modern UI**: A fast, clean interface that stays out of your way.

## How to Use

1.  **Download**: Download `BloxMulti.exe` from the **Releases** tab.
2.  **Add Accounts**: Click the "+" button in the sidebar to log in.
3.  **Launch**: Pick an account (or hold Shift to pick multiple), enter a Place ID, and click **Launch**.
4.  **Options**: Right-click accounts or games to pin them, remove them, or open a browser window for that account.

## Privacy & Security

BloxMulti handles your logins through the official Microsoft WebView2 runtime. All your data is stored locally on your own computer; nothing is ever sent to external servers.
